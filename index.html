About Mistriva Holidays Kerala

Where your trips become memorable…

Kerala’s trusted travel partner for unforgettable journeys

Welcome to Mistriva Holidays, Kerala’s trusted travel partner for unforgettable journeys.

Based in Munnar, Idukki, we are a passionate team of travel lovers who believe that a trip is not just about reaching a destination. It’s about the laughter on the way, the mist on the hills, the stories you bring back, and the memories that stay with you forever.

We specialize in crafting customized Kerala tour packages for families, friends, couples and corporate groups. Whether it’s a relaxing honeymoon in Munnar, an adventurous group trip to Vagamon, a soulful visit to Athirapally waterfalls, or a peaceful houseboat cruise in Alleppey, we plan every detail with care, honesty and love.
